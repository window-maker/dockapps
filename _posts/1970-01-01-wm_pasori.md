**wm_pasori** is a Window Maker dockapp that displays the balance of Suica IC cards via a Sony PaSoRi (Tested RC-370, etc.) USB read.

This dockapp uses **libpafe** ( = 0.6) and **libdockapp** ( = 0.0.8).
